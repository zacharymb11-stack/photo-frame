Alarms

Alarms ring while Photo Frame is open on the screen (any screen: photos, scenes or clock). For it to work: keep the iPad plugged in, set Auto-Lock to Never, turn the volume up and make sure the iPad isn't on silent. After opening Photo Frame, tap the screen once so the iPad allows sound.
